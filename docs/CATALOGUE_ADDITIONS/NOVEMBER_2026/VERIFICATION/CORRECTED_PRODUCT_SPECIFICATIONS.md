## 1. Specification Methodology

All specifications below reflect strictly verified manufacturer documentation:
- **Manufacturer-Confirmed:** Data points directly sourced from official engineering datasheets, user manuals, and factory documentation.
- **Distributor-Reported / Secondary Lab:** Empirical parameters verified by accredited third-party laboratories or national distribution partners, explicitly labeled to maintain evidentiary transparency.
- **Excised / Prohibited Claims:** All speculative chemical claims (including soluble calcium precipitation and pH 8.2 buffering assertions for sand), unverified biological strains, unproven enzymatic timings, and unsupported geographical statements have been removed.
