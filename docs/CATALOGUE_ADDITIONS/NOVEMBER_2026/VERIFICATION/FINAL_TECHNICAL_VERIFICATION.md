### Product 1: Blue Treasure Calcium Sand #2 — 5 kg
* **Catalogue Identifier:** `blue-treasure-calcium-sand-2`
* **Manufacturer:** Qingdao Sea-Salt Aquarium Technology Co., Ltd. (Brand: Blue Treasure)
* **Primary Source Page:** `https://www.aquaseasalt.net/blue-treasure-2-aquarium-calcium-sand/`
* **Source Type:** Primary Manufacturer

#### A. Verified Primary Specifications
1. **Grade Identification:** Grade #2.
2. **Granulometry / Grain Size:** 1.0–2.0 mm (Manufacturer documented range: 1–2 mm; distinct from Grade #1 [0.3–1.0 mm] and Grade #3 [3.0–4.0 mm / 2.0–3.0 mm depending on production run]).
3. **Primary Chemical Composition:** Natural Calcium Carbonate ($\text{CaCO}_3$) substrate.
4. **Pack Size:** 5 kg sealed plastic bag with official Grade #2 markings.
5. **Suitability:** Formulated for marine, reef, and hard-water freshwater setups (e.g., African cichlid biotopes).

#### B. Corrections & Unsupported Claim Removals
* **EXCISED:** Specific pH-triggered buffering claim (*"dissolves trace calcium as pH approaches 8.1–8.3"*). Manufacturer documentation does not specify a threshold pH activation trigger.  
  *Correction:* Replaced with the documented fact that the substrate slowly precipitates soluble calcium, carbonates, and trace elements, helping to increase carbonate hardness (KH) and naturally buffer aquarium water stability around pH 8.2.
* **EXCISED:** Claim of *"guaranteed prevention of anoxic pockets"*. Granulometry of 1–2 mm facilitates water percolation compared to fine silt, but absolute prevention of anaerobic zones is unverified by manufacturer documentation and depends on tank water movement and husbandry.
* **EXCISED:** Claim of *"guaranteed resistance to high-velocity flow"*. While heavier than sugar-fine sand (0.3–0.5 mm), the manufacturer makes no engineering warranty regarding high-velocity wavemaker displacement.

### Product 2: Blue Treasure SPS Sea Salt
* **Catalogue Identifier:** `blue-treasure-sps-salt`
* **Manufacturer:** Qingdao Sea-Salt Aquarium Technology Co., Ltd. (Brand: Blue Treasure)
* **Primary Source Pages:**  
  - Product Profile: `https://www.aquaseasalt.net/`
  - Directions for Use: `https://www.aquaseasalt.net/wp-content/uploads/`
* **Source Type:** Primary Manufacturer & National Authorised Distributor

1. **Target Application:** Specially formulated for Small Polyp Stony (SPS) reef environments, targeting enhanced calcification and skeletal expansion.
2. **Raw Material Provenance:** Food-grade raw materials containing natural trace elements, special vitamins, and protective colloids. Sodium chloride ($\text{NaCl}$) is vacuum-crystallised and purified from deep underground brine sourced from the Bohai Bay and Yellow Sea regions.
3. **Official Mixing Ratio:** **6.7 kg** of SPS Sea Salt mixed with **50 gallons (190 Litres)** of fresh water (RO/DI water recommended) until completely dissolved. This equals approximately **1 kg per 28.36–28.5 Litres** of water.
4. **Target Temperature & Specific Gravity:**  
   - Operating Temperature: 23°C – 25°C (73°F – 77°F).  
   - Target Specific Gravity: **1.024 – 1.026** at 23–25°C.
5. **Recommended Water Change Regimen:** Weekly 10% partial water change.
6. **Manufacturer Packaging & Model Codes:**
   - **HZY001:** 3.35 kg bag (6 bags/carton)
   - **HZY004:** 3.35 kg bucket
   - **HZY002:** 6.7 kg bag (3 bags/carton) / 6.7 kg bucket
   - **HZY006:** 20 kg bag / 20 kg bucket

#### B. Separation of Distributor-Reported / Secondary Lab Values
* **Distributor-Reported Chemical Parameters:** Independent distributor test analyses (including Aquariums India and international reef hobbyist lab tests) report nominal concentrations at 1.025 S.G. of:
  - Calcium ($\text{Ca}$): ~430–450 ppm
  - Magnesium ($\text{Mg}$): ~1380–1450 ppm
  - Strontium ($\text{Sr}$): ~8–10 ppm
  - Alkalinity ($\text{KH}$): ~8.2–9.0 dKH
* *Editorial Note:* On the public catalogue, these figures are qualified as *distributor/hobbyist lab-tested nominal parameters at 1.025 S.G.*, while the primary manufacturer specification guarantees *elevated Calcium, elevated Magnesium, Strontium, and trace elements above standard marine salt formulations*.
* *Display String Alignment:* The owner's preferred `6.67 kg` display string is presented on the storefront alongside the manufacturer's exact `6.7 kg` specification in technical documentation.

### Product 6: Bubble Magus Mini Q Nano Protein Skimmer
* **Catalogue Identifier:** `bubble-magus-mini-q`
* **Manufacturer:** Jiyang Aquarium / Bubble Magus Co., Ltd.
* **Primary Source Page:** `https://bubble-magus.net/products/bubble-magus-mini-q` & Official Factory Instruction Manual
* **Source Type:** Primary Manufacturer & Factory Manual

#### A. Investigation of Dimension Discrepancy
* **Identified Discrepancy:** The initial research dossier reported dimensions of $82 \times 51 \times 226\text{ mm}$, whereas certain distributor documentation listed approximately $82 \times 53 \times 225\text{ mm}$.
* **Root-Cause Analysis:**
  - Official factory metric engineering drawings: **82 mm × 53 mm × 225 mm**.
  - Certain Western distributors converted these metric dimensions into rounded imperial measurements: $3.2" \times 2.0" \times 8.9"$.
  - Secondary retailers then converted the rounded imperial figures back into metric: $2.0" \times 25.4\text{ mm} = 50.8\text{ mm} \approx 51\text{ mm}$, and $8.9" \times 25.4\text{ mm} = 226.06\text{ mm} \approx 226\text{ mm}$, resulting in the artificial $82 \times 51 \times 226\text{ mm}$ figure.
* **Resolution:** The authoritative factory specification of **82 mm × 53 mm × 225 mm** (Footprint: 82 × 53 mm, Height: 225 mm) is adopted.

#### B. Verified Primary Specifications
1. **Model Designation:** Mini Q (MINI-Q).
2. **Pump & Power:** Integrated **DC5V needle-wheel pump**, consuming **2 Watts**. Powered via standard universal **USB interface** (supplied with 100–240V, 50/60Hz AC wall adapter outputting DC5V, 1A).
3. **Rated Aquarium Capacity:** Up to **75 Litres (20 Gallons)**.
4. **Operating Water Depth:** Minimum operating water depth of **16 cm (approx. 6.3 inches)**.
5. **Mounting Specification:** Integrated glass bracket accommodating aquarium glass thicknesses up to **12 mm (1/2 inch)**.
6. **Form Factor:** Engineered specifically for rear chambers of All-In-One (AIO) aquariums and compact nano reef sumps. Features rotary dial water-level adjustment and micro air silencer.
7. **Model Distinction:** Strictly differentiated from BM-QQ1, BM-QQ2, BM-QQ3, and Mini Q Plus.
