### Product 1: Blue Treasure Calcium Sand #2 — 5 kg
* **Product ID:** `blue-treasure-calcium-sand-2`
* **Brand:** Blue Treasure (Qingdao Sea-Salt Aquarium Technology Co., Ltd.)
* **Category:** `rock-sand` (Substrate & Sand)
* **Owner Selling Price:** ₹1,300
* **Grade:** #2 Medium Grade (1–2 mm grain size)
* **Chemical Composition:** High-purity Calcium Carbonate ($\text{CaCO}_3$) harvested from natural aragonite reef sand beds.
* **Function & Aquascaping Utility:**
  - Designed for marine and reef aquarium landscaping.
  - Granulometry (1–2 mm) prevents compaction and anoxic gas pockets while remaining heavy enough to resist dispersion by high-velocity wavemakers and flow pumps.
  - Slowly buffers aquarium water by releasing calcium and carbonate ions as pH approaches 8.1–8.3, providing passive alkalinity support.
* **Packaging Specification:** 5 kg sealed plastic bag with official Blue Treasure #2 identification.
* **Research Sources:**
  - Manufacturer Official: `https://www.aquaseasalt.net/blue-treasure-2-aquarium-calcium-sand/`
  - Technical Verification: Confirmed Grade #2 represents 1–2 mm (distinct from Grade #1 0.5–1 mm fine sugar sand and Grade #3 2–3 mm coarse sand).

### Product 2: Blue Treasure SPS Sea Salt (Multi-Variant Parent)
* **Product ID:** `blue-treasure-sps-salt`
* **Brand:** Blue Treasure (Qingdao Sea-Salt Aquarium Technology Co., Ltd.)
* **Category:** `salt-chemistry` (Salts & Chemistry)
* **Base Product Price:** ₹950 (Displaying entry variant price)
* **Variant Structure:**
  1. `sps-salt-3-35kg`: 3.35 kg Pack / Bucket — ₹950
  2. `sps-salt-6-7kg`: 6.67 kg (6.7 kg manufacturer spec) Pack / Bucket — ₹1,900
  3. `sps-salt-20kg`: 20 kg Bucket — ₹4,500
* **Formulation & Biochemical Characteristics:**
  - Specifically engineered for the intensive calcification demands of Small Polyp Stony (SPS) corals (*Acropora*, *Montipora*, *Seriatopora*, *Pocillopora*, *Stylophora*).
  - Contains elevated, bio-available levels of Calcium ($\text{Ca}^{2+}$: 430–450 ppm), Magnesium ($\text{Mg}^{2+}$: 1380–1450 ppm), and Strontium ($\text{Sr}^{2+}$: 8–10 ppm) with essential trace elements (potassium, iodine, molybdenum, iron, cobalt).
  - Fast-dissolving, synthetic formulation free of unwanted phosphates, nitrates, silicates, and environmental heavy metals.
* **Mix Rate & Operational Parameters:**
  - Recommended mixing ratio: approximately 1 kg of SPS salt per 28.5 litres of purified reverse osmosis (RO/DI) water.
  - Target Specific Gravity: 1.024–1.026 at 23–25°C.
  - Target Salinity: 33.5–35.0 ppt.
* **Research Sources:**
  - Manufacturer Official: `https://www.aquaseasalt.net/`
  - Authorized National Distributor: `https://www.aquariumsindia.com/shop/marine-supplies/marine-sea-salt/blue-treasure-sps-sea-salt/`
  - Packaging Note: Manufacturer literature defines 3.35 kg bag, 6.7 kg bucket/bag, and 20 kg heavy bucket. The owner's display preference of "6.67 kg" is seamlessly accommodated in the variant title alongside the manufacturer's exact 6.7 kg specification.

### Product 4: Quantum Mixed Macro Probiotic Salt — 22 kg
* **Product ID:** `quantum-mixed-macro-probiotic-salt-22kg`
* **Brand:** Quantum Aqua / Quantum USA
* **Category:** `salt-chemistry` (Salts & Chemistry)
* **Owner Selling Price:** ₹6,200
* **Pack Size:** 22 kg heavy-duty sealed tub
* **Biochemical Formulation & Target Audience:**
  - Specifically designed for "Mixed Reef" aquaria housing both SPS and LPS corals, soft corals, and marine fish.
  - Explicitly differentiated from *Quantum SPS Macro Probiotic Salt* (which targets 8.5–9.0 dKH for pure SPS growth). The requested *Mixed Macro Probiotic Salt* targets a balanced **8.0 dKH** alkalinity at 1.025 S.G. to safeguard LPS corals (*Euphyllia*, *Acanthastrea*, *Micromussa*, *Trachyphyllia*) from alkalinity burn while maintaining adequate carbonate hardness for SPS calcification.
  - Mix rate: approximately 35 grams per litre of RO/DI water.
  - Quality Assurance: Every batch is tested via ICP-OES (Inductively Coupled Plasma Optical Emission Spectroscopy) in Quantum's Australian laboratories; an individual batch ICP analysis certificate is included in every tub.
* **Research Sources:**
  - Manufacturer Official: `https://quantumusa.us/products/mixed-macro-probiotic-salt%E2%84%A2`
  - Technical Laboratory Verification: ICP batch guarantee confirmed on product packaging and official technical datasheet.
