## 1. MAPPING METHODOLOGY & COMPLIANCE RULES

To safeguard customer trust and maintain strict legal compliance with Indian consumer protection standards (Consumer Protection Act 2019, Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules 2020) and international copyright law:

1. **No Conflation of Sources:** External reviews from third-party retailers (e.g., Bulk Reef Supply, Amazon, Charterhouse Aquatics) must **never** be presented as Marine Creatures customer reviews.
2. **Dedicated Presentation Banner:** Any external feedback presented to shoppers must carry the clear label:  
   `"EXTERNAL PRODUCT FEEDBACK — Aggregated from independent marine aquarium communities & authorized retailers"`
3. **Fair Use Summaries vs. Verbatim Copying:** Because third-party platform terms of service prohibit commercial reproduction of user reviews, Marine Creatures must display **concise factual summaries of community sentiment** alongside direct source links, rather than republishing verbatim user comments.
4. **Zero External Image Copying:** Under no circumstances will user-submitted photographs from third-party platforms be copied or hosted locally.
5. **Strict Quarantine Policy for Live Fish:** Live marine fish (`marine-life`) have **zero** external reviews. Because individual specimens are quarantined and shipped directly by Marine Creatures, reviews for live animals must **only** originate from verified first-party purchasers who received the specific live arrival.
